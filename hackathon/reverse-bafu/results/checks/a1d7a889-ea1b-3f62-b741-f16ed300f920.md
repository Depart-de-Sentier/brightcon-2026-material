# Check: Naphtha, APME mix, at refinery (a1d7a889-ea1b-3f62-b741-f16ed300f920)

target `bafu-2026` vs explicit model `a1d7a889-ea1b-3f62-b741-f16ed300f920-disagg` and hybrid `a1d7a889-ea1b-3f62-b741-f16ed300f920-hybrid`

**Strategy:** S2 — template transfer: BAFU's own refinery unit process for the same product, at the same location. S2, not S1: no report in the bundle prints an inventory for the APME mix (the locate response records the search). What makes this one reachable anyway is that BAFU-2026 already contains a unit process of the same product at the same location - 'Naphtha, at refinery' [RER], 33 technosphere inputs and 126 elementary flows, the ecoinvent refinery model documented in 2007 - Crude oil - Jungbluth.pdf. The two datasets differ in provenance, not in product: the target is PlasticsEurope's own cumulated refinery figure for the naphtha its members buy ('product out of refining process of oil and gas', ecoSpold technology field), the sibling is ecoinvent's model of the same European refinery slate. Substituting one for the other is exactly the template transfer S2 describes, and it is a 1:1 substitution because both are 1 kg of naphtha at the refinery gate - there is no region, scale or vintage to adapt, so nothing is calibrated and there is no free amount in this spec at all. What the check then measures is a real question about the database: how far apart are PlasticsEurope's and ecoinvent's naphtha? Caveat: the target is the APME *mix*, which may be sourced differently from the ecoinvent RER slate, and nothing in the bundle says how.

AFTER THE CHECK. The question this spec was written to answer - how far apart are PlasticsEurope's and ecoinvent's naphtha? - has an answer, and it is: far. Fossil CO2 agrees to -16 %, but every other headline flow of the APME profile is far higher than the ecoinvent refinery model produces: methane -98.9 %, NMVOC -98.3 %, nitrogen oxides -84.8 %, carbon monoxide -82.3 %, sulfur dioxide -74.7 %. In other words the APME naphtha carries roughly 50-100x the methane and NMVOC of ecoinvent's, at comparable CO2. Overall: 0 of the 50 largest kilogram flows within +-10 %, 4 of 1668 determined flows. The two datasets are not interchangeable, and this spec's value is that it says so with numbers rather than leaving the substitution to be made silently downstream.
