Two steps, both named by the technology field. Condensation: 2 C6H7N (aniline, 186.258) + CH2O (formaldehyde, 30.026) -> C13H14N2 (methylenedianiline, 198.269) + H2O (18.015); 216.284 = 216.284. Phosgenation: C13H14N2 + 2 COCl2 (197.820) -> C15H10N2O2 (MDI, 250.257) + 4 HCl (145.832); 396.089 = 396.089. Per kg of MDI: 186.258/250.257 = 0.74426 kg aniline, 30.026/250.257 = 0.11998 kg formaldehyde, 197.820/250.257 = 0.79046 kg phosgene; co-products 0.58273 kg HCl and 0.07199 kg water. Mass balance of the whole model: 1.6547 kg in, 1.6547 kg out. Molar masses from the IUPAC 2021 standard atomic weights: H 1.008, C 12.011, N 14.007, O 15.999, S 32.06, Cl 35.45, Na 22.990. The hydrogen chloride co-product (0.583 kg per kg of MDI, more than half the product mass) is recorded in this note and given no credit, because nothing in the bundle states an allocation. Amounts are fixed, not fitted: they are the stoichiometric floor at 100 % conversion. Known gaps: the hydrochloric acid catalyst of the condensation step and the chlorobenzene/monochlorobenzene solvent of the phosgenation, both recycled and neither quantified anywhere; and the polymeric-MDI fraction, which the dataset does not distinguish.
