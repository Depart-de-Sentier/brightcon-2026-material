# Check: Polystyrene, general purpose, GPPS, at plant (1235438d-3385-3bd1-8921-17951c10c464)

target `bafu-2026` vs explicit model `1235438d-3385-3bd1-8921-17951c10c464-disagg` and hybrid `1235438d-3385-3bd1-8921-17951c10c464-hybrid`

**Strategy:** S3 — top-down model from the dataset's own process description: monomer by mass balance, utilities from the BAFU chemicals template and calibrated. NOT S1. No report in the bundle prints an inventory for this dataset. The input list comes from the dataset's own ecoSpold technology field, 'polymerization out of ethylene and benzene by free radical processes'. The field describes the chain back to the cracker ('out of ethylene and benzene'), which is the route to styrene; the monomer of general-purpose polystyrene is styrene and nothing else. Chain-growth (addition) polymerisation: the repeat unit and the monomer have the same empirical formula, so 1 kg of polymer carries 1 kg of monomer plus whatever is lost. No conversion-loss figure exists anywhere in the bundle, so 1.000 kg is used and the true value can only be higher - fixed, not fitted. Same convention as the committed HDPE / LDPE / LLDPE / PP / PVC specs. The monomer is itself an unflagged PlasticsEurope eco-profile (results/system_terminated_extended.csv, detected_by=structure), so this model terminates on an aggregated dataset and the structural check says so - correctly. It is linked to the BAFU dataset rather than to the sibling rebuild so that this spec's check measures only this spec's evidence; re-point it with the input's `sandbox` field to chain the rebuilds together. 

AFTER THE FIT. All five utilities went to zero (electricity 5.5E-11 kWh, heat 6.9E-9 MJ, both transports ~1E-9 tkm, the plant 3.6E-21 units), so the node is exactly '1 kg of aggregated styrene' - which is what the evidence says and is the honest outcome. Its headline flows are close: fossil CO2 +29 %, methane -2 %, biogenic CO2 +7 %, SO2 -8 %, NOx -13 %, NMVOC -9 %. Its overall flow agreement is nevertheless 25 of 1667 (1 %), and the reason is not the chemistry: 'Styrene, at plant' declares 0.5615 kg of hazardous waste to incineration per kg, against 0.0070 kg for this dataset, 0.0088 for HIPS and 0.0022 for benzene. A polymer's cradle-to-gate profile cannot contain 80x less waste than the monomer it is made of, so one of the two numbers is wrong, and the mass balance of the rest of the family says it is the styrene one. Every flow that hazardous-waste chain feeds - calcite +1010 %, gravel +1120 %, rock salt +3094 %, chloride to water +4690 % - is that single exchange, not this model. See exports/README.md, 'The APME eco-profiles the type=2 flag missed'.
