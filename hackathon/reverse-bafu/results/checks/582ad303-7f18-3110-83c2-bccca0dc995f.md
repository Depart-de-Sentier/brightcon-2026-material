The cited report (Rajabihamedani 2025) prints no unit process - it documents only the post-processing of a PlasticsEurope system process (see specs/evidence/582ad303-.../response-0-locate.json). The inventory here is transcribed from Althaus 2007, ecoinvent report No. 8, Tab. 35.3, which documents the multi-output process 'ethylene oxide, in ethylene glycol plant' that ecoinvent v2 used for this same dataset name, and allocated to MEG with the report's own 85 % price-times-mass factor over its 0.825 kg of MEG. The transcription is verified, not assumed: applying the same table with the DEG factor (7 %/0.1 kg) and the TEG factor (8 %/0.04 kg) reproduces BAFU's own 'Diethylene glycol, at plant' and 'Triethylene glycol, at plant' unit processes on all eleven exchanges the table covers - exactly on nine of them (ethylene oxide 0.4921 kg, electricity 0.2639 kWh, chemical plant 2.8E-10, waste heat 0.945 MJ, acetaldehyde 9.8E-07, ethylene 4.2E-06, water 0.0042 m3, and the TEG column 1.406 / 0.754 / 8E-10) and to 0.15 % on the two four-digit ones (COD/BOD 2.013E-04 against 2.016E-04, TOC/DOC 5.248E-05 against 5.250E-05). The twelfth exchange, the waterborne water emission, is not in Tab. 35.3 at all and is taken from the co-products' own 0.95 ratio. This is S2 and not S1 because the BAFU dataset being rebuilt is the PlasticsEurope 2019 eco-profile (reference year 2023), a different data generation from the 1997-2000 ecoinvent v2 inventory transcribed here; the structure is the same product's, the vintage is not. Steam is calibrated because Tab. 35.3 omits it and sec. 35.6.2 says so explicitly, and the infrastructure is calibrated between 0 and the transcribed 4.1212E-10 because the ethylene-oxide precursor already carries one chemical plant of its own.

Sec. 35.5 notes the wider IPPC range: 'the glycol products typically consists of 70 - 95% w/w of MEG' - the 82.5 % split is one point in it.
- 2007 - LCI chemicals - Althaus.pdf — sec. 35.6.2 Energy usage 'It must be assumed that some steam is utilized for glycol distillation. No figures are given. Electrical energy consumption is given as 0.377 kWh / kg in (BUWAL 232).' The steam input is therefore in the model but calibrated, unbounded - the report names it and refuses to quantify it.
- bafu-2026: Diethylene glycol, at plant [RER] and Triethylene glycol, at plant [RER] — cross-check of the transcription Both are 3-input unit processes still in BAFU-2026 and are the co-products of the very table transcribed here. Tab. 35.3 x 0.07/0.1 gives ethylene oxide 0.4921 kg, electricity 0.2639 kWh, chemical plant 2.80E-10 unit, water 0.0042 m3, waste heat 0.945 MJ, acetaldehyde 9.8E-07, ethylene 4.2E-06, BOD 2.016E-04, COD 2.016E-04, DOC/TOC 5.25E-05 - which is exactly what BAFU's DEG carries (0.4921 / 0.2639 / 2.8E-10 / 0.0042 / 0.945 / 9.8E-07 / 4.2E-06 / 2.013E-04 / 2.013E-04 / 5.248E-05). Same for TEG at 0.08/0.04 (EO 1.406, electricity 0.754, plant 8E-10). Two consequences: the transcription is verified, and the report's COD of 2.88E-01 kg is a typo for 2.88E-04 (the co-products carry BOD = COD), which is what is used here. The co-products also fix the water balance, 0.00399/0.0042 = 0.95 of the water input leaving as a waterborne emission; Tab. 35.3 gives only the input.
- 2025 - LCI plastics - Rajabihamedani.pdf — sec. 3.6 Infrastructure 'For all datasets, the inventory regarding infrastructure contributions was checked and supplemented. For the implementation, the dataset "Chemical plant, organics {RER}U" is used. The quantity is the standard amount for production of chemicals of 4.0E-10 p.' The BAFU target indeed carries 4.0E-10 p, and Tab. 35.3 gives the same 4.00E-10 unit - the two vintages agree on this one input.
