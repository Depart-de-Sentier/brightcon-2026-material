No report in the bundle prints an inventory for this dataset. The input list comes from the dataset's own ecoSpold technology field, 'polymerization out of ethylene and benzene by free radical processes'. The field describes the chain back to the cracker ('out of ethylene and benzene'), which is the route to styrene; the monomer of general-purpose polystyrene is styrene and nothing else. Chain-growth (addition) polymerisation: the repeat unit and the monomer have the same empirical formula, so 1 kg of polymer carries 1 kg of monomer plus whatever is lost. No conversion-loss figure exists anywhere in the bundle, so 1.000 kg is used and the true value can only be higher - fixed, not fitted. Same convention as the committed HDPE / LDPE / LLDPE / PP / PVC specs. The monomer is itself an unflagged PlasticsEurope eco-profile (results/system_terminated_extended.csv, detected_by=structure), so this model terminates on an aggregated dataset and the structural check says so - correctly. It is linked to the BAFU dataset rather than to the sibling rebuild so that this spec's check measures only this spec's evidence; re-point it with the input's `sandbox` field to chain the rebuilds together. 

AFTER THE FIT. All five utilities went to zero (electricity 5.5E-11 kWh, heat 6.9E-9 MJ, both transports ~1E-9 tkm, the plant 3.6E-21 units), so the node is exactly '1 kg of aggregated styrene' - which is what the evidence says and is the honest outcome. Its headline flows are close: fossil CO2 +29 %, methane -2 %, biogenic CO2 +7 %, SO2 -8 %, NOx -13 %, NMVOC -9 %. Its overall flow agreement is nevertheless 25 of 1667 (1 %), and the reason is not the chemistry: 'Styrene, at plant' declares 0.5615 kg of hazardous waste to incineration per kg, against 0.0070 kg for this dataset, 0.0088 for HIPS and 0.0022 for benzene. A polymer's cradle-to-gate profile cannot contain 80x less waste than the monomer it is made of, so one of the two numbers is wrong, and the mass balance of the rest of the family says it is the styrene one. Every flow that hazardous-waste chain feeds - calcite +1010 %, gravel +1120 %, rock salt +3094 %, chloride to water +4690 % - is that single exchange, not this model. See exports/README.md, 'The APME eco-profiles the type=2 flag missed'.

RESULT OF THE COMPARISON. Chaining makes it worse, and that is the point of the measurement: 8 of 1668 determined flows within +-10 % (0 %, median |delta| 10123 %) against 25 of 1667 (1 %) when the same spec links BAFU's aggregated styrene. The reason is structural, not a defect of the styrene rebuild: the target is a 1990s industry survey that reports ~146 substances and no infrastructure, while the rebuilt styrene reaches ethylbenzene, benzene, the European grid and a chemical plant and therefore produces ~1670. 'Terminate on the database' buys transparency - the burden becomes traceable to ethylbenzene dehydrogenation - and costs agreement with a target whose background was never ecoinvent's. Anyone choosing between the two nodes should choose on that basis, not on the score.
