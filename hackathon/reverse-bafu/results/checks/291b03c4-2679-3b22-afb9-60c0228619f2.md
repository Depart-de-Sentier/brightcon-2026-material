**Strategy:** S3 — top-down model: the acetone-cyanohydrin route the dataset names, amounts from the reaction equation with molar masses, utilities calibrated. NOT S1. No report in the bundle prints an inventory. The dataset's ecoSpold technology field, 'product out of hydrogen cyanide and acetone', is the classic ACH route, and BAFU carries the intermediate as its own dataset ('xx Acetone cyanohydrin, at plant'), so the model is built one step at a time rather than collapsing the two: acetone + HCN -> ACH is the cyanohydrin spec, and this spec is the amidation/esterification step. C4H7NO (acetone cyanohydrin, 85.106) + H2SO4 (98.072) + CH3OH (methanol, 32.042) -> C5H8O2 (methyl methacrylate, 100.117) + NH4HSO4 (ammonium bisulfate, 115.103); mass balance 215.220 = 215.220. Per kg of MMA: 85.106/100.117 = 0.85006 kg of acetone cyanohydrin, 98.072/100.117 = 0.97957 kg of sulphuric acid and 32.042/100.117 = 0.32005 kg of methanol, with 1.14973 kg of ammonium bisulfate as co-product. Molar masses from the IUPAC 2021 standard atomic weights: H 1.008, C 12.011, N 14.007, O 15.999, S 32.06, Cl 35.45, Na 22.990. Amounts fixed, not fitted. The ammonium bisulfate co-product weighs more than the product itself and nothing in the bundle gives an allocation or says whether the plant regenerates the acid, so no credit is taken and that is the single largest uncertainty in this spec. The acetone cyanohydrin input is itself an unflagged eco-profile (rebuilt here), so the structural check flags a dependency.
