# Check: Polyethylene, LLDPE, granulate, at plant (df878822-0deb-3a59-a6b4-396d94a3b654)

target `bafu-2026` vs explicit model `df878822-0deb-3a59-a6b4-396d94a3b654-disagg` and hybrid `df878822-0deb-3a59-a6b4-396d94a3b654-hybrid`

**Strategy:** S3 — top-down model: monomer by mass balance, input list from the dataset's own process description, energy calibrated. NOT S1. The cited report prints no inventory. The input list is the one the BAFU dataset itself declares in its ecoSpold technology/includedProcesses field, and the monomer amount is a mass balance, not a measurement: addition (chain-growth) polymerisation adds the monomer to the chain unchanged, so the repeat unit and the monomer have the same empirical formula and 1 kg of polymer needs 1 kg of monomer plus whatever is lost. No loss figure exists anywhere in the bundle, so 1.000 kg is used and the real value can only be higher - this is the one place where the model is knowingly at a bound. Electricity and steam are named by the dataset and quantified nowhere, so they are fitted, unbounded; they are the whole gate-to-gate difference between the polymer and its monomer. The alpha-olefin comonomer (typically a few per cent of 1-butene or 1-hexene) is a gap: no comonomer is named in the dataset's own text and no dataset for one is identified, so it is carried inside the ethylene.
**Calibrated inputs:** Electricity, medium voltage, production ENTSO-E, at grid, Steam, for chemical processes, at plant, Chemical plant, organics [0–4e-10], Transport, freight, rail, Transport, freight, lorry, fleet average
