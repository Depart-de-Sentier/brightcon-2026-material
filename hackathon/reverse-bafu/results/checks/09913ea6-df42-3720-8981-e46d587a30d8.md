**Strategy:** S3 — top-down model from the dataset's own process description: monomer by mass balance, utilities from the BAFU chemicals template and calibrated. NOT S1. No report in the bundle prints an inventory for this dataset. The input list comes from the dataset's own ecoSpold technology field, 'production by different types of polymerization out of MMA'. The field names the monomer outright; 'sheet' is the cast form. Chain-growth (addition) polymerisation: the repeat unit and the monomer have the same empirical formula, so 1 kg of polymer carries 1 kg of monomer plus whatever is lost. No conversion-loss figure exists anywhere in the bundle, so 1.000 kg is used and the true value can only be higher - fixed, not fitted. Same convention as the committed HDPE / LDPE / LLDPE / PP / PVC specs. The monomer is itself an unflagged PlasticsEurope eco-profile (results/system_terminated_extended.csv, detected_by=structure), so this model terminates on an aggregated dataset and the structural check says so - correctly. It is linked to the BAFU dataset rather than to the sibling rebuild so that this spec's check measures only this spec's evidence; re-point it with the input's `sandbox` field to chain the rebuilds together. The BAFU datasets for beads and for sheet carry the same technology text and differ only in their cumulative vectors, and nothing in the bundle says how the two forms differ in process, so the two specs differ only in what the fit puts into the free utilities. That is stated here rather than hidden.
