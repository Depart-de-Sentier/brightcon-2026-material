# Check: Polycarbonate, at plant (53117f8b-9311-37b3-bf5b-6e14b2ae711c)

**Strategy:** S3 — top-down model: the two monomers the dataset names, amounts from the polycondensation equation with molar masses, utilities calibrated. NOT S1. No report in the bundle prints an inventory. The dataset's own ecoSpold technology field is unusually specific - 'production by interfacial polycondesation out of phosgene and bisphenol A' - and names both reagents and the process variant, and BAFU carries a real unit process for each ('Bisphenol A, powder, at plant', 7 inputs; 'Phosgene, liquid, at plant', 7 inputs), so this model terminates on the database with no aggregated dependency at all. Amounts are the polycondensation stoichiometry: C15H16O2 (bisphenol A, 228.291) + COCl2 (phosgene, 98.910) -> -[C16H14O3]- (carbonate repeat unit, 254.285) + 2 HCl (72.916); mass balance 327.201 = 327.201. Per kg of polycarbonate: 228.291/254.285 = 0.89777 kg of bisphenol A and 98.910/254.285 = 0.38897 kg of phosgene, releasing 0.28675 kg of HCl. In the interfacial process the HCl is taken up by caustic soda, 2 NaOH per repeat unit = 2 x 39.997 / 254.285 = 0.31459 kg. Molar masses from the IUPAC 2021 standard atomic weights: H 1.008, C 12.011, N 14.007, O 15.999, S 32.06, Cl 35.45, Na 22.990. The caustic soda is not named by the technology field; it is included because the field says *interfacial* polycondensation, which by definition runs the bisphenol as its disodium salt in an aqueous phase against phosgene in dichloromethane, and the HCl released must be neutralised. Two named-but-unquantified pieces are left out and recorded as gaps: the dichloromethane solvent (recycled, so its net consumption is a make-up rate nothing prints) and the chain terminator/catalyst. All three amounts are fixed, not fitted: they are the stoichiometric floor at 100 % conversion, so the true values can only be higher.
