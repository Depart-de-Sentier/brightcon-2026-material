- bafu-2026 ecoSpold metadata of the target (includedProcesses / technology) — includedProcesses / technology 'The VCM production process, upstream processes like ethylene and chlorine production, electricity, and steam production as well as transportation of feedstock and waste treatment.' - this is the whole input list the dataset declares, and it is what the model contains.
- 2007 - LCI chemicals - Althaus.pdf — sec. 34.5.3 Thermal cracking of EDC 'C2H4 + Cl2 -> C2H4Cl2 (Chlorination of ethylene to EDC) / C2H4Cl -> CH2CHCl + HCl (Cracking of EDC to form VCM) / C2H4 + 1/2 O2 + 2HCl -> C2H4Cl2 + H2O (Oxychlorination route to EDC)'. 'Often all the HCl generated in the cracking section is reused in producing EDC by oxychlorination. Plants that exhibit this characteristic and also do not export EDC are called “balanced”. The balanced process is the common process used as a Best Available Technology benchmark.' Summing the three gives the balanced overall reaction 2 C2H4 + Cl2 + 1/2 O2 -> 2 C2H3Cl + H2O, in which the HCl cancels - which is why VCM is modelled here from ethylene and chlorine directly and not from 1.583 kg of EDC (that route would double-count the chlorine, because half of it comes back as HCl).
- 2007 - LCI chemicals - Althaus.pdf — sec. 34.5.1 EDC by direct chlorination of ethylene 'Yield on ethylene 96-98% / on chlorine 98%' - the midpoint 97 % and the 98 % are applied to the stoichiometric amounts.
- bafu-2026: Ethylene dichloride, at plant [RER] — cross-check of the transport convention The one BAFU unit process for the same chemistry carries 0.6 tkm rail + 0.1 tkm lorry per kg, chlorine, liquid, production mix 0.735 kg and ethylene 0.295 kg - it reproduces Althaus Tab. 34.3 exactly, which is the check that this way of reading the report gives back the dataset it built.
