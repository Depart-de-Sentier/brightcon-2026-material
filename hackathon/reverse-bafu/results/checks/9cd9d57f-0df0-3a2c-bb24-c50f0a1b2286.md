**Strategy:** S3 — top-down model, deliberately incomplete: adipic acid by the polycondensation equation; the diamine has no dataset in BAFU-2026. NOT S1, and not complete either - read this before using the node. No report in the bundle prints an inventory. The dataset's ecoSpold technology field names both monomers - 'production out of hexamethylene diamine and adipic acid' - and the polycondensation equation gives both amounts exactly: Polycondensation: C6H10O4 (adipic acid, 146.142) + C6H16N2 (hexamethylenediamine, 116.208) -> -[C12H22N2O2]- (nylon-6,6 repeat unit, 226.320) + 2 H2O (36.030); 262.350 = 262.350. Per kg of nylon 6,6: 146.142/226.320 = 0.64573 kg of adipic acid and 116.208/226.320 = 0.51347 kg of hexamethylenediamine, releasing 0.15921 kg of water. Molar masses from the IUPAC 2021 standard atomic weights: H 1.008, C 12.011, N 14.007, O 15.999, S 32.06, Cl 35.45, Na 22.990. Only one of the two can be linked: 'Adipic acid, at plant' [RER] is a real 7-input BAFU unit process, but BAFU-2026 contains no hexamethylenediamine dataset at all, and no adiponitrile or hexanedinitrile either - searched by name over all 11,947 datasets. So this model carries 0.646 kg of the 1.159 kg of monomer the reaction needs, i.e. 56 % of the input mass and none of the nitrogen; the missing 0.513 kg of diamine is not a rounding gap but half the chemistry, and it is why the flow agreement of this node must not be read as a quality statement about the method. It is published rather than withheld because the adipic-acid half is exact and because the missing half is named, quantified and traceable: anyone who adds a hexamethylenediamine dataset can complete it by adding one line.
