The dataset's ecoSpold technology field names three reagents - 'production out of phosgene, toluene and hydrogen' - which is the toluene -> dinitrotoluene -> toluenediamine -> TDI route and nothing else; hydrogen is only ever used to reduce the nitro groups, so the field implies the nitration and therefore the nitric acid, which is added here for that reason and flagged. Three steps. Nitration: C7H8 (toluene, 92.141) + 2 HNO3 (126.024) -> C7H6N2O4 (dinitrotoluene, 182.135) + 2 H2O (36.030); 218.165 = 218.165. Hydrogenation: C7H6N2O4 + 6 H2 (12.096) -> C7H10N2 (toluenediamine, 122.171) + 4 H2O (72.060); 194.231 = 194.231. Phosgenation: C7H10N2 + 2 COCl2 (197.820) -> C9H6N2O2 (TDI, 174.159) + 4 HCl (145.832); 319.991 = 319.991. Per kg of TDI: 92.141/174.159 = 0.52906 kg toluene, 126.024/174.159 = 0.72361 kg HNO3, 12.096/174.159 = 0.06945 kg hydrogen, 197.820/174.159 = 1.13585 kg phosgene; co-products 0.83733 kg HCl and 0.62064 kg water. Mass balance: 2.45797 kg in, 2.45797 kg out. Molar masses from the IUPAC 2021 standard atomic weights: H 1.008, C 12.011, N 14.007, O 15.999, S 32.06, Cl 35.45, Na 22.990. Amounts are fixed, not fitted: the stoichiometric floor at 100 % conversion. One input is an aggregated dataset - 'Toluene, liquid, at plant', itself an unflagged PlasticsEurope eco-profile whose own rebuild is blocked by the BTX allocation (see its locate response) - so the structural check flags a dependency, correctly. The sulphuric acid of the mixed-acid nitration is regenerated and is a recorded gap; so are the 0.837 kg of HCl co-product per kg of TDI, for which nothing in the bundle states an allocation. BAFU's nitric-acid dataset is declared per kg of HNO3, not of the 50 % solution - checked against 'Adipic acid, at plant', which carries 0.431 kg of it against a stoichiometric 0.862 kg, i.e. exactly half, consistent with the NOx recovery that route runs on.
