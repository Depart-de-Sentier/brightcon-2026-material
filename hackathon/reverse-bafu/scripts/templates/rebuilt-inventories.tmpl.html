Agreement is measured on the cumulative inventory itself — flow by flow, no impact assessment, so the verdict does not depend on a characterisation method. Three views of the same comparison: the 50 largest kilogram flows, the share of the original’s total kilogram mass that the rebuilt process reproduces within ±10 %, and every flow counted alike (which the long tail of small background flows dominates). The hybrid node of each equals the original flow for flow by construction. "Calibrated amounts" were fitted by bounded least squares with the input list fixed; inputs at a bound signal the report's range disagrees with the original vector.
