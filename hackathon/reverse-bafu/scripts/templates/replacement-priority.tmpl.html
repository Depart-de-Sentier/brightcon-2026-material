How the score is built

importance of s in category c = the sum, over every other dataset a in BAFU, of the share of a's impact that passes through s anywhere in its supply chain

Why a sum of shares

A count of consumers treats a dataset that is 90 % driven by the system process like one where it contributes 0.1 %. Summing the shares weighs each dependant by how much of its result actually passes through. The ranking score is the mean over the 16 headline EF 3.1 categories, so climate is not weighted against toxicity. Checked against ordinary LCA runs: the pipe that the ranking says is 90 % HDPE comes out at 89.9 %.

What it does not see

Every dataset counts once; how often each is used in real studies is not known here. The score measures how much of the rest of BAFU a system process sits inside, so a final product that nothing else consumes scores zero, even if practitioners select it directly. The 138 are the system processes we found and investigated, not necessarily all of BAFU's.
