Reading this honestly

The originals were built on older data

Every rebuild links to today's BAFU-2026 unit processes; the system processes were computed with the background of their time. Even BAFU's own dis-aggregated epoxy, swapped in one for one, emits 2.7× the fossil CO₂ of the epoxy system process. A mismatch here is therefore not only an error in our rebuild.

Rebuilds tend to run low

Where the evidence names an input without an amount, the fit often pushes it to the bottom of its range; missing utilities then show up as missing emissions. That is the blue side of the grid, and it is strongest for the S3 top-down models.

Where the data comes from

scripts/flow_comparison.py computes every number on this page into results/flow_comparison.json: one matrix factorisation over every spec in specs/, round-off flows excluded via lci.determined_flows.
